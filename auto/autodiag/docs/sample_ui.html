3. Diagnosis

limit driving
Cylinder 1 is misfiring at idle on an otherwise healthy engine. The most likely cause is a failing ignition coil on that cylinder, with the spark plug a close second. The flashing check engine light means raw fuel reached the catalyst; keep trips short until repaired.

Ranked causes

#1Cylinder 1 ignition coil failure48%
A single-cylinder misfire at warm idle with normal fuel trims and normal manifold vacuum points to a cylinder-specific ignition fault rather than a shared fuel or air problem. Coil-on-plug coils on this engine family are the most frequent single-cylinder failure, and the misfire worsening when cold fits a coil breaking down at higher secondary voltage demand.
obd P0301 Cylinder 1 misfire stored; P0300 only pending, so the pattern is cylinder-specific.
obd LONG_FUEL_TRIM_1 LTFT +4.7% and STFT +5.5% are within normal range, arguing against a lean misfire.
manual "For a single-cylinder misfire on a modern coil-on-plug engine, in rough order of frequency: ignition coil, spark plug, fuel injector..."
[c1] Misfire Isolation Workflow (P0300 to P0308) section "Common causes by frequency"
For a single-cylinder misfire on a modern coil-on-plug engine, in rough order of frequency: ignition coil, spark plug, fuel injector, valve or compression problem, wiring or connector to the coil or injector, intake gasket leak near that runner.
symptom Shake at stoplights and rough idle match an idle-load misfire.
against No P0351 coil circuit code, so the primary circuit is intact; a coil can fail on the secondary side without setting one.
#2Worn or fouled spark plug, cylinder 127%
At 128,400 miles the plugs may be past their service interval. A plug with an opened gap raises voltage demand and misfires first at idle and under cold-start enrichment. The swap test separates this from the coil in one step.
obd P0301 Same cylinder-specific evidence as the coil.
manual Plug reading guide: wet with fuel indicates no spark, worn gap beyond specification calls for replacement.
general_knowledge Iridium plugs on this engine are typically due around 100,000 miles.
against Plug wear usually affects all cylinders similarly; a single-cylinder fault slightly favors the coil.
#3Restricted or weak fuel injector, cylinder 115%
A partially clogged injector leans out one cylinder and produces an idle misfire that improves as airflow rises. The mildly positive fuel trim is consistent but not diagnostic.
manual Injector balance test: a drop much smaller than the others indicates a restricted injector.
against Bank fuel trim is only +4.7%; a significantly restricted injector usually pushes trims higher.
#4Low compression on cylinder 1 (valve or ring)10%
Mechanical causes are less common but must be ruled out if ignition and fuel test good. Normal manifold vacuum at idle argues against a large leak but does not exclude a single tight valve.
obd INTAKE_PRESSURE 33 kPa at idle indicates normal overall vacuum.
manual Compression should be within 10 percent across cylinders; wet test separates rings from valves.

Next tests, in order

1. Coil swap test ~30 min · basic hand tools, scan tool · source
Engine off and cool. Move the cylinder 1 coil to cylinder 3 and the cylinder 3 coil to cylinder 1; mark them. Clear codes, idle 10 to 15 minutes under the freeze-frame conditions, and re-read codes.
SplitsCylinder 1 ignition coil failure, Worn or fouled spark plug cylinder 1, Restricted or weak fuel injector cylinder 1, Low compression on cylinder 1 If faultyMisfire code moves to P0303. Replace the coil. If OKP0301 returns on cylinder 1. Coil is good; proceed to the plug.
2. Inspect and swap spark plug ~20 min · plug socket, gap gauge · source
Remove the cylinder 1 plug. Read the tip for fuel wetting, oil, or fouling and check the gap against specification. Swap with cylinder 3 and repeat the drive cycle.
SplitsWorn or fouled spark plug cylinder 1 If faultyMisfire moves with the plug, or the plug is visibly worn or fouled. Replace all four. If OKMisfire stays on cylinder 1 with a clean, correctly gapped plug.
3. Injector balance and audible check ~25 min · stethoscope, scan tool with injector balance · source
Listen at each injector for a steady click. Run the injector balance test and compare the cylinder 1 pressure drop to the others.
SplitsRestricted or weak fuel injector cylinder 1 If faultyCylinder 1 drop is markedly smaller than the rest, or the injector is silent. If OKAll four drops within about 10% of each other.
4. Compression test, all cylinders ~45 min · compression gauge · source
Remove all plugs, disable fuel, crank each cylinder the same number of strokes. If cylinder 1 is low, add a small amount of oil and retest.
SplitsLow compression on cylinder 1 If faultyCylinder 1 more than 10% below the others. If OKAll cylinders within 10%.

Missing information

  • Misfire counter per cylinder (Mode 06) to confirm cylinder 1 is the only contributor and P0300 is not masking a second cylinder.
  • Service history: when were plugs or coils last replaced?
  • Whether the misfire is present at 2500 rpm in neutral, which separates idle-only from load-dependent causes.

Safety

  • A flashing check engine light indicates a catalyst-damaging misfire rate. Limit driving until repaired.
  • Let the engine cool before handling coils; the exhaust manifold is adjacent on this engine.
